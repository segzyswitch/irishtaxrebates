How You Will Receive Your Tax Rebate From 2026 Onwards

New Customers: Apply now Existing Customers: Apply For Additional Rebate

Your Tax Rebate Will Now Be Paid Directly to You (From 2025)

Last updated: 20 October 2025

From January 2025, Revenue will begin paying approved tax rebates directly to taxpayers’ bank accounts, rather than transferring the funds to your tax agent (e.g., Irish Tax Rebates). This process will streamline the rebate process and allow you to receive your tax rebate quicker than ever.

At Irish Tax Rebates, we fully support this change — and we’ll make the transition simple. Your service with us does not change. We’ll still look after your claim from start to finish.

What’s Changing — and What Stays the Same

  • Changing: When your rebate is approved, Revenue will pay it directly to your bank account.
  • Not Changing: Irish Tax Rebates will still review your taxes, prepare your claim, submit to Revenue, and keep you updated.
  • Your Fee: Our fee arrangement remains as agreed and is handled separately. Our fee will just be paid after you receive the rebate, instead of being taken from source.

Two Quick Actions So You Don’t Miss a Payment

1️⃣ Wait to Hear from Irish Tax Rebates
We’ll contact you shortly with full details on how to update your information securely. You don’t need to take any action until you receive our direct instructions. This will ensure your rebate and bank details are processed correctly under the new system.

2️⃣ Prepare for Your 2025 Rebate
We’re already preparing 2025 claims so we can file as soon as possible in January. Uploading your expenses early helps us ensure you receive your rebate quickly.

Submit Additional Expenses

What Can I Claim for 2025?

You can further increase your rebate potential by submitting additional expenses, to claim tax credits like:

  • 🏠Rent Tax Credit or Mortgage Interest Tax Credit
  • 🩺Medical & Dental Expenses (the portion not covered by insurance)
  • 💻Working From Home costs

FAQ’s about this process

Will Irish Tax Rebates still be my agent?

Yes. We will still be able to prepare and manage your claim with Revenue on your behalf.

Will my rebate amount change?

No. The change affects only where the approved refund is paid — not how your rebate is calculated.

When will I hear from Irish Tax Rebates about updating my details?

We’ll be in touch over the coming weeks with secure instructions on how to update your details and ensure your 2025 rebate is processed correctly.

When will 2025 claims be filed?

Revenue opens for 2025 claims in January. If you submit your 2025 expenses now, we’ll be ready to submit this as early as possible in the New Year.

  • Upload your 2025 expenses so your claim is ready to file early in the new year.
